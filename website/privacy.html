1. What we collect

When you sign in, we receive your name and email address from your chosen sign-in provider (Google). We store which content you've saved and reacted to, tied to your account, so your saved list and preferences persist across devices.

2. What we don't collect

We do not collect payment information, precise location, or sensitive health details beyond anything you choose to share with us directly (for example, if you contact us).

3. How we use your data

Solely to operate REVA: showing your saved items, recognizing your account, and understanding — in aggregate and anonymized form — which content resonates, so we know what to make more of.

4. Cookies and local storage

This website uses essential cookies/local storage to keep you signed in and to remember that you've seen our cookie notice. See our Cookies Policy for details.

5. Data storage & security

Data is stored with Google Firebase and protected by access controls restricting who can read or write it. We do not sell user data to third parties.

6. Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as REVA grows. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
